KEY INSIGHTS

What is shaping Industrial Tech M&A?

01

A stable market with a changing deal mix

Industrial technology transaction volumes remained relatively stable between 2022 and 2025, while buy-and-build strategies and Physical AI gained importance.

02

Deal activity does not necessarily translate into higher valuations

The most active industrial technology segments are not necessarily those commanding the highest valuation multiples.

03

End-market exposure matters

The research identifies substantial valuation differences between companies serving cyclical industries and those exposed to structurally growing end markets.
